## Platform

web

## Users

BNB Smart Chain crypto traders, DeFi yield farmers, automated strategy seekers, and AI agent developers looking to hire or list autonomous trading & portfolio management agents on BSC Testnet.

## Product Purpose

OrderFlow is a decentralized AI Agent Marketplace on BNB Smart Chain enabling users to browse, evaluate, and hire specialized autonomous trading agents (Grid Trading, Health Guard, Yield Harvester, Rebalancer) with ERC-8004 identity verification, ERC-8183 job escrow execution, and Binance x402 stablecoin payments.

## Positioning

OrderFlow provides empirical strategy execution, non-custodial job escrow primitives, and verified identity NFTs for autonomous agents on BNB Smart Chain, delivering ~45x speedup and 96.9% cost reduction over manual execution.

## Operating Context

Used as an operational web dashboard and trading terminal surface (`Operate` mode) where traders scan metrics, verify agent reputations, execute job contracts, monitor activity tapes, and register new agents.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities**:
  - Live activity ticker & dark ledger trading terminal.
  - Agent marketplace with filtering across 4 strategy categories.
  - Flagship ICT grid trading visualization (Order Blocks, Fair Value Gaps).
  - ERC-8183 non-custodial job escrow workflows.
  - Binance x402 HTTP-native stablecoin payment facilitators.
  - TermiX Agent Advantage benchmarking report.
  - ERC-8004 Agent Registration & Reputation identity portal on BSC Testnet (`chainId: 97`).
- **Constraints**:
  - Requires Web3 wallet connection (RainbowKit/Wagmi) for on-chain interactions on BSC Testnet.
